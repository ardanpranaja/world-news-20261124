# Sources — The World Today, Tuesday 24 November 2026

Every item on the front page was cross-checked against at least two independent
reports before it was set in type. Attributions and date nuances are noted where
a claim rests on a single interested source or an earlier ruling.

## Lead — Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Libya
- Facts: Ukrainian naval drones sank the Russian corvette **Merkury** in
  Benghazi port and hit the cargo vessel Angara. President Zelensky said seven
  Ukrainian-made naval drones were used. The strike happened before dawn on
  Sunday 22 November; Zelensky publicly confirmed it on Monday 23 November and
  it remained the leading story on 24 November. The UN mission in Libya called
  for an immediate, independent, transparent investigation; Libya's eastern
  administration and the Tripoli-based foreign ministry both demanded
  explanations from Kyiv.
- Reported by: WSJ, CNN, Le Monde, The Times, ABC/AP-style reporting, UN
  mission statement coverage. (Some sources transliterate the ship as
  "Merkuriy".)

## Markets — Huang returns to Nvidia; markets soar
- Facts: Nvidia's board reinstated founder Jensen Huang as CEO and president
  on Monday 23 November, after pressure/intervention by President Trump.
  Huang had resigned 11 days earlier. Nvidia rose about 31%; the S&P 500
  gained 4.73%, the Nasdaq Composite 6.51%, and Japan's Nikkei 3.91% in the
  following Asian session.
- Reported by: Reuters, CNN, Le Monde, WSJ, Barron's, The Times, Investopedia.

## World court — 14 nations back genocide ruling
- Facts: The International Court of Justice ruled on **19 November** that
  Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya and breached its obligations
  to prevent and punish genocide. On 23 November, **14 countries** affirmed
  the ruling and called on Myanmar to comply. Britain separately recognised
  the genocide and sanctioned five senior Myanmar military figures (asset
  freezes and travel bans). The front page therefore headlines the backing,
  not the ruling itself.
- Reported by: AFP-derived reports, UK/FCDO-derived reporting, Just Security.

## Climate — COP31 ends without a deal
- Facts: COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye ended without adopting the overarching
  "Antalya Pact". Negotiations ran about 44 hours overtime. The EU and more
  than 130 countries rejected a draft that omitted explicit fossil-fuel
  transition language. Detailed reports date the conclusion to Sunday
  22 November, with reaction continuing into 23–24 November; the front page
  makes no harder date claim than the summit having ended.
- Reported by: The Times, The Energy Year, Anewz, Asia Daily, The Arabian
  Post, ITDC.

## Europe — NATO on alert after missiles fall in Poland
- Facts: Polish prosecutors said three Russian Kh-101 missiles landed in
  Poland on Friday 20 November. NATO agreed to forward-deploy AWACS
  early-warning aircraft to cover Poland and the eastern flank. A Russian
  drone briefly entered Polish airspace during a later attack on western
  Ukraine. Russia told Estonia its actions were "on the verge of triggering
  open war".
- Attribution: the nuclear-armed satellite is a **report** (German
  media/intelligence reporting), denied by the Kremlin and not independently
  verified — the front page says "amid reports of", never that it exists.
- Reported by: Reuters (two reports), The Independent, Baltic news digests.

## In brief
- **Hormuz** — Iran's IRGC says three tankers hit mines despite a US escort.
  This is a **claim by Iran's IRGC**, carried by IRNA/Iranian state-linked
  outlets; US Central Command had not confirmed it in the reports checked.
  Separately, UKMTO-derived reporting counted record attack levels around
  Hormuz (30+ verified incidents in October, at least 22 in November).
- **Indonesia** — Authorities confirmed on 24 November that the death toll
  from the fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Cikande,
  Serang, Banten (fire began 21 November) had reached **26**. Contemporaneous
  reports disagreed on how many workers remained missing, so the page says
  only that the search continues. Reported by Xinhua, Straits Times/AP-style
  reporting, People.
- **Space** — China named Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali
  to the Shenzhou-24 crew on 24 November, with launch scheduled for
  25 November; he is set to become the first foreign astronaut to visit
  Tiangong and the first Pakistani in space. The page uses "set to visit" —
  he had not flown yet. Reported by Reuters, Nature, Xinhua/AP-style
  reporting.
- **Health** — The United States lost its measles-elimination status (PAHO
  announcement reported 17 November); Pennsylvania announced its ninth
  measles-related death on 23 November, with about 1,638 cases across
  46 counties. Reported by CBS-derived reporting, The Hill, CIDRAP.
- **AI** — UN Secretary-General António Guterres, speaking at the Bridgetown
  Special Summit on AI Safety in Barbados on 23 November, urged member states
  to develop, within a year, a globally inclusive multilateral framework for
  AI risk management with independent oversight — a proposal, not an adopted
  framework. Reported by UN coverage via Diplomacy.edu, Al Jazeera-derived
  reports.
